---
name: Historical ledger evidence
description: Evidence limits when importing legacy customer accounting records.
---

Treat an uploaded general-ledger statement as evidence of its named accounts, dated entries, references and balances only. Do not infer service scope, tax treatment, project completion, quotations, or unspecified allocations of receipts and returns.

**Why:** The user initially selected importing confirmed information only. They subsequently explicitly confirmed that the source invoice totals include 15% VAT and requested one independent project per invoice, editable operational invoices, payment tracking and PDF downloads. This approval establishes the tax basis and administrative project grouping, but not service details, execution completion, or unspecified credit allocations.

**How to apply:** Preserve the original account snapshot while keeping operational records separate. Unactivated historical references remain review-only. Activated invoices may be updated and downloaded using the owner's confirmed VAT basis, with a clearly labelled aggregate service description until actual details are supplied. The new project is an administrative follow-up file, not evidence of execution. Link source receipts only by explicit invoice references; leave other receipts and returns as customer credits for explicit manual allocation. Allocation consumes an existing credit and never creates new cash or additional revenue.
